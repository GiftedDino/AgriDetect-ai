Precision Agriculture Paradigm

Transforming Crop Health with Artificial Intelligence

Plant diseases and insect pests are responsible for substantial annual yield losses worldwide—accounting for an estimated 20% to 40% reduction in global crop production according to the Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO).

Traditional disease diagnosis relies upon manual field scouting and expert visual estimation, which is labor-intensive, subjective, and prone to misidentification between fungal, bacterial, and viral pathologies. Our system leverages state-of-the-art computer vision models trained on tens of thousands of validated leaf and pest specimens to identify symptoms at early stages, preventing catastrophic epidemics before they compromise entire harvests.

Pathogen Differentiation

Disentangles bacterial leaf blights from fungal spots and viral mosaics.

Integrated Pest Management

Prioritizes biological & cultural controls over unneeded broad-spectrum pesticides.

Scientifically Categorized Diagnosis

Unlike naive classifiers that classify every input into a generic disease category, our platform categorizes every inference into 6 strict agricultural classes:

  • A. Healthy Crop: Optimal photosynthetic vigor and clean foliage. Healthy
  • B. Fungal Disease: Rusts, powdery/downy mildews, late & early blights. Fungal
  • C. Bacterial Disease: Water-soaked spots, ooze, black arm, and vascular blights. Bacterial
  • D. Viral Disease: Mosaic mottling, leaf curling, vector transmission. Viral
  • E. Pest Infestation: Armyworms, aphids, whiteflies, borers, leaf miners. Pest
  • F. Unknown / Unsupported: Rejects non-plant and unconfident inputs. OOD

How the Detection System Works

Designed for swift in-field diagnosis through mobile browser camera access or lab photo uploads.

Upload Crop or Pest Image

Take a photo directly with your camera or drag and drop a leaf, fruit, or pest image. High-resolution input gives optimal feature extraction.

2

Receive Identification & Advisory

Agronomic Value

Benefits of AI in Modern Crop Protection

Bridging academic phytopathology with practical field decisions to enhance agricultural sustainability.

Early Disease Interception

Detects minute lesions, chlorotic halos, and early pest clusters before systemic necrosis develops, shrinking yield reduction from 40% to under 5%.

Judicious Chemical Application

Distinguishes viral infections (where fungicides are totally ineffective) from fungal blights, preventing wasteful pesticide expenditure and runoff.

IPM & Biological First

Supplies clear Integrated Pest Management guidance with biological parasitoids, cultural crop rotations, and neem formulations before chemical rescue.

Epidemiological Forecasting

Integrates temperature and relative humidity thresholds to assess fungal sporulation risks (e.g. Wallin / Blitecast models for potato late blight).

Academic & Research Rigor

Built for agricultural students, incorporating full scientific binomial names (e.g. Phytophthora infestans), pathogen taxonomy, and peer-reviewed references.

Printable Advisory Reports

Generate formatted Diagnostic Advisory Sheets for extension agents to share directly with smallholder farmers in rural agricultural districts.
