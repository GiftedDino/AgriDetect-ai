AI-Based Crop and Pest Disease Detection System: Deep Transfer Learning & Calibrated Phytopathological Inference

An end-to-end machine learning study addressing early-stage foliar disease diagnosis, insect pest identification, and out-of-distribution anomaly filtering using depthwise separable convolutional neural networks.

Primary Research Domains:
Computer Vision, Agricultural Phytopathology, Applied Entomology
Benchmark Datasets:
PlantVillage, PlantDoc, IP102 Pest Repository
Deployment Backbone:
MobileNetV2 Transfer Learning with Linear Bottlenecks

1. Abstract & Problem Statement

Phytopathogenic micro-organisms (fungi, bacteria, viruses) and destructive herbivorous insect pests constitute a persistent threat to global food security. In resource-constrained rural agrarian environments, smallholder farmers often lack prompt access to certified plant pathologists. Misidentification frequently leads to improper chemical applications—such as spraying costly systemic fungicides on viral mosaic diseases (where fungicides possess zero biochemical efficacy) or confusing bacterial water-soaked leaf streaks with fungal leaf spots.

This project provides a robust, lightweight, and calibrated diagnostic system capable of identifying crop health conditions across 12 major economic crops (Rice, Wheat, Maize, Tomato, Potato, Cotton, Apple, Grape, Pepper, Sugarcane, Groundnut, Soybean) and 10 major insect pests, strictly segmenting outcomes into six mutually exclusive agronomic categories: Healthy Crop, Fungal Disease, Bacterial Disease, Viral Disease, Pest Infestation, and Unknown or Unsupported Image.

2. Curated Benchmark Datasets

PlantVillage
54,306 Images • 38 Classes

High-resolution laboratory-captured foliar images on uniform backgrounds. Provides primary baseline weights for foliar blight concentric rings, viral curling, and pristine healthy leaves.

PlantDoc
2,598 In-Field Images • 13 Species

Complex field-condition imagery featuring occluded leaves, direct sunlight glare, natural soil backgrounds, and multiple overlapping lesions for robust generalization.

IP102 Pest Dataset
75,222 Images • 102 Pest Classes

Hierarchical agricultural pest dataset covering all insect life cycle stages (eggs, larvae, nymphs, pupae, and adult moths/beetles) and distinctive plant damage symptoms.

4. Neural Network Architecture: Inverted Residuals & Linear Bottlenecks

In mobile agricultural diagnostics, edge inference latency and parameter footprint are critical. MobileNetV2 replaces standard convolutional layers with Depthwise Separable Convolutions, decomposing standard $D_K \times D_K$ convolutions into a depthwise spatial filtering stage and a $1 \times 1$ pointwise projection stage. This reduces computational complexity by a factor of:

Computational Reduction Ratio = \(\frac{1}{N} + \frac{1}{D_K^2} \approx \frac{1}{9}\) of standard convolution
